Score = 100 × (0.35·momentum7 + 0.25·momentum30 + 0.40·age bonus) × liquidity multiplier. Momentum saturates at ±30%; the age bonus peaks ~240 days after release; liquidity scales the total between 0.5× and 1×. 180d momentum is shown for context but doesn't feed the score. Knobs live in card_scoring.py.

The score and the numbers below always use the full price history, no matter which range the graph is showing.

Every nightly refresh adds one more day and nothing is ever thrown away, so the 90-day view keeps filling in over time even when the price source only hands back a week of history. A card added today starts with whatever history the source gives it and builds from there.

If 7D and 30D show the same number, this card only has one or two prices so far, so both windows fall back to the same baseline. That usually sorts itself out after the next nightly refresh, now that missing days get filled in automatically.

What each column means
Card
Name, printed number, and (for Pokémon) an EN/JP badge for English vs Japanese.
Set
The expansion/set the card was printed in.
Rarity
The card's printed rarity or variant (e.g. Special Illustration Rare).
Game
Pokémon or One Piece.
Price
The most recent known market price.
7 day
Percent price change over the last 7 days. Green = up, red = down.
30 day
Percent price change over the last 30 days.
180 day
Percent price change over the last 180 days. Shown for context only — it isn't part of the Score below.
Age hidden by default
Days since the card's set released. Scoring favors the window roughly 8 months after release — past the initial hype, before the card gets genuinely scarce.
Liquidity hidden by default
How often the price has actually moved in the last 30 days (0–100%). A card that's been flat for weeks scores low here even if the price itself is high.
Score
0–100. Rolls 7d/30d momentum and age together, then scales the total by liquidity. 🟢 65+ is hot, 🟡 45–65 is worth watching, 🟠 under 45 is cold — it's a signal to look closer, not a buy/sell call.
