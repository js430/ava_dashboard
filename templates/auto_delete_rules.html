Auto-delete rules
A rule removes a new For Sale post when its title or first message contains the words you list, and sends the seller a message saying why and when they can post again.
Rules only check brand-new posts. A new rule won't remove posts that are already up, and ending or deleting a rule won't bring back posts it already removed. Changes apply to the very next new post — nothing needs restarting. In Search Of is never touched.

What to look for
A post is removed if any group matches. A group matches when the post has every word in the first box and none of the words in the second. Capital letters don't matter, and a word matches inside longer words — 30th catches “Pokemon 30Th Anniversary”. Press Enter or comma to add a word.
